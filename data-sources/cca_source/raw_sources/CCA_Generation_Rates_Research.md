Where the most current TOU-specific PDF could not be fetched, the best available data is provided with clear notation.

**Seasons**: Summer = June 1–September 30, Winter = October 1–May 31 (unless otherwise noted for legacy EV plans: Summer = May 1–Oct 31, Winter = Nov 1–Apr 30).
